# Data attribution

The `*.png` tiles are cutouts of the Digitized Sky Survey 2 (red plates),
fetched via the CDS **hips2fits** service
(https://alasky.cds.unistra.fr/hips-image-services/hips2fits).
The DSS is based on photographic data obtained using the Oschin Schmidt
Telescope (Palomar) and the UK Schmidt Telescope, produced at the Space
Telescope Science Institute. Compressed survey data © original institutions;
used here for demonstration/education. Regenerate with `python fetch_tiles.py`.

These image files are third-party data and are **not** covered by this
repository's MIT license; they remain subject to the DSS/STScI terms above.
